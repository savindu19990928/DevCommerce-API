# System Features

This document outlines the core features of the system that are available and manageable.

## Authentication & User Management
*   **User Authentication:** Secure login and session management using JWT (JSON Web Token).
*   **User Management:** Full administrative capabilities to manage users and assign roles.
*   **Granular Permissions:** Advanced permission engine allowing delegation of specific capabilities (view, add, change, delete) to staff members on a per-model or per-object basis.
*   **Password Management:** Features for users to securely reset forgotten passwords and change current passwords.
*   **Contact Verification:** System to send and verify 6-digit OTP codes via email or phone to validate user contact information.
*   **Address Management:** Capabilities for users to manage their billing and shipping addresses.

## Catalog & Products
*   **Dynamic Homepage:** Curated product lists including popular, trending, on-sale, and personalized recommendations.
*   **Product Browsing:** Paginated viewing of active products with filtering options by brand, category, and preorder status.
*   **Product Management:** Comprehensive control over product details including pricing, cost margins, stock levels, expiration dates, pre-order status, and unit of measurement.
*   **Product Reviews:** Ability for verified customers to leave ratings and comments on products.

## Order Management
*   **Order History & Tracking:** View past and current orders, with public tracking available via secure tokens.
*   **Checkout Options:** Support for both registered user checkout and full guest checkout (including automatic calculation of estimated arrival dates).
*   **Order Receipts:** Generation and retrieval of PDF receipts, with the ability to trigger automated receipt emails or SMS.
*   **Order Fulfillment:** Workflow to mark orders as packed by assigning specific product serial numbers, which automatically updates the order status to in-transit.
*   **Shopping Cart & Wishlist:** Complete management of user shopping carts and wishlists.
*   **Coupons:** Creation and management of discount coupons.
*   **Call Requests:** Customers can request a phone call regarding specific orders (e.g., for late deliveries or issues).

## Support & Customer Care
*   **Warranty Registration:** Ability to register product serial numbers for warranty purposes.
*   **Refunds:** Customers can initiate refund requests which can then be processed and approved by staff.
*   **Customer Care Cases:** System to open, view, and manage support tickets or cases.
*   **Case Communications:** Integrated messaging and chat functionality within active support cases to communicate with customers.

## Inventory & Stock Management
*   **Stock Movements:** Logging of all physical inventory changes including additions, removals, damages, and manual adjustments.
*   **Serial Number Tracking:** Management of individual product serial numbers and tracking their real-time state (e.g., in stock, reserved, packed, sold).

## Marketing
*   **Recommendation Tags:** Creation and management of tags to dynamically group and recommend related products.

## Reports & Analytics
*   **System Analytics:** Access to key reporting metrics including gross revenue, order volume, product catalog volume, and low stock alerts.
